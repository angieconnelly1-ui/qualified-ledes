## Why profiles get suspended

Google suspends or disables profiles that it believes break its Business Profile guidelines. It rarely tells you exactly which rule. The usual causes:

- **Keywords stuffed into the business name**, such as "Smith Plumbing Best Plumber Richmond".
- **An address that does not qualify**, like a P.O. box or a UPS Store mailbox.
- **A problem with a manager's Google account.** If someone who manages your profile has a suspended or spam-flagged account, your profile can be caught up in it.
- **Duplicate or misleading listings**, or a service-area business showing a home address it should hide.

## What to do first

1. **Do not create a new profile.** Google's guidance is clear on this, and a duplicate can make things worse.
2. **Check the profile against the guidelines.** Remove keywords from the name, confirm the address and categories, and make sure the hours and phone number are accurate.
3. **Check who has access.** Remove any manager you do not fully trust or recognize.
4. **Gather evidence.** Business registration, a business license, a tax certificate or a utility bill. The name and address on each must match the profile exactly.

## How to appeal

1. Open the Google Business Profile **appeals tool** and sign in with the Google Account that manages the profile.
2. Select the profile, then the decision you want to appeal.
3. Add your documents and a short, factual explanation of what you fixed.
4. Submit. When you open the evidence form, Google gives you 60 minutes to finish, so have your files ready first.

Treat it as your one shot: Google says appeals can only be submitted once. Have someone else read your profile and evidence before you send it.

You can track the result in the appeals tool. The statuses are Submitted, Approved, Not Approved, Can't be appealed and Eligible for appeal.

## If the appeal is denied

Google may do an additional review only after a denial. Use the local appeals form to request it and include any evidence you did not send the first time. Businesses in the European Economic Area may have extra redress options.

## If your Google Account is restricted

A restricted account suspends every profile it manages and blocks you from creating or claiming new ones. Appeal the account restriction first from your Google Account, then appeal each profile.

## Preventing the next one

- Keep the business name exactly as it appears on your signage and paperwork.
- Make changes in small steps, not all at once.
- Review who has manager access every few months.
- Keep a folder of current registration and utility documents so an appeal takes minutes, not days.
